Privacy

Privacy information

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Shopwise processes information needed to provide shop-management functionality. This page describes the current application behavior and is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

Information processed

  • Account information such as name, email address, and authentication identifiers.
  • Shop, product, inventory, staff, sales, payment-status, return, and report records entered through the service.
  • Operational information such as request identifiers, errors, and security logs.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, enforce shop access, provide requested MiniPOS features, process supported payments, generate reports, deliver configured emails, prevent abuse, and diagnose service problems.

Service providers

Shopwise currently relies on Supabase for authentication and database services, Railway for backend hosting, Vercel for frontend hosting, Stripe for card-payment processing, and Resend for report-email delivery. Their handling of data is also governed by their respective policies.

Payment information

Card details are collected by Stripe's browser components and are not stored in the Shopwise database. Shopwise stores the business records and provider references needed to reconcile payment status.

Access and security

Backend authorization and database policies scope business data to the appropriate shop. No internet service can promise absolute security; account holders should use unique passwords and protect their login sessions.

Questions

For privacy questions, use the channel listed on the contact page.
